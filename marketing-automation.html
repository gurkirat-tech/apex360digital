Our Process

How We Deliver Results

A structured, repeatable process delivering measurable outcomes from week one and compounding growth every month.

01
Discovery & Audit

We map your current lead flow, sales process, and existing tools — identifying manual bottlenecks, dropped leads, and automation gaps before designing a single workflow.

02
Workflow Mapping

Every trigger, condition, and action mapped out and reviewed with your team before we build — so the automation mirrors exactly how you sell and market, not a generic template.

03
Build & Integration

Workflows built natively inside your CRM and marketing platform, fully integrated with your existing tools, forms, and data sources — with staging access throughout the build.

04
Testing & QA

Every trigger and branch tested with real scenarios before go-live — checking edge cases, timing, and data accuracy so nothing breaks once leads start flowing through.

05
Training & Handoff

Live walkthroughs and documentation so your team can confidently monitor, tweak, and extend the workflows themselves — no dependency on us to make small changes.

06
Launch & Optimisation

Post-launch monitoring and monthly optimisation reviews included with every project — refining triggers and sequences as your data shows what's actually working.
